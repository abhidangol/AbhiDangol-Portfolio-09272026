Process is the key to success

A streamlined process built on collaboration, creativity and precision, so the finished project matches your vision.

  1. Free strategy01

    A free strategy to start

    Before our free, no-obligation call, I send a short form that takes about five minutes to fill in.

    • Define your goals and target audience
    • Talk through your deliverables
    • Receive a free strategy document
  2. Concept02

    Onboarding

    Happy with the strategy? On our second call I onboard you to my project management system so everything is clear.

    • Create milestones and timelines
    • Collect assets and information from you
    • Set up your payment plan
  3. Design03

    Let the magic happen

    With creativity and precision I bring your project to life, refining every detail so it is captivating, cohesive and impactful.

    • Project design and development
    • QA testing and security checks
    • Ready to review, with revisions
  4. Delivery04

    Launch & maintenance

    Once everything is approved and tested, I hand over source files and access, set up backups, launch, and stay on for upkeep.

    • Preparation for launch
    • Handover of files
    • Post-delivery support and maintenance

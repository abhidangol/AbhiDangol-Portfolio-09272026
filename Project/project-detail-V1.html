Product Design

A live workspace that gives logistics teams one clear view of every shipment, supplier and warehouse, replacing scattered spreadsheets and status emails.

Challenge

Planners tracked orders across five tools, so delays surfaced only after customers noticed.

Approach

I mapped how planners actually work, then built one dashboard around exceptions instead of lists.

Outcome

Teams now spot at-risk shipments early and act from a single screen.

-40%Time spent on status checks
5→1Tools replaced by one workspace
92%Task success in usability tests
01/03Spot the risk

Three flows that matter

The product is built around the moments where a planner has to decide fast.

  1. Alerts01

    Spot the risk

    Delays float to the top with a reason attached, so nothing important hides in a long table.

    • Exception-first home view
    • Plain-language delay reasons
  2. Resolve02

    Act on it

    Reroute, reassign or message a supplier without leaving the shipment, in two clicks.

    • Inline reroute options
    • Supplier messages in context
  3. Reports03

    Report back

    Weekly performance builds itself, ready to share with leadership and customers.

    • One-click shareable reports
    • Trends by supplier and route
